Nontobeko Nkabinde

Nontobeko Nkabinde is the listed director of N.A Nkabinde Attorneys in Johannesburg. Her publicly listed profile records an LLB qualification, admission as an attorney in 2020 and the opening of the firm in 2021.

Her practice-area listings include family law, divorce, civil and criminal litigation, wills, estates, personal injury and related matters. She is listed as speaking English, isiZulu and isiXhosa.

Clients are encouraged to contact the practice for current information on consultations and appropriate assistance.
